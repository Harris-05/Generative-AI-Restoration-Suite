## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a hyper-modern, precision-engineered aesthetic built for cutting-edge AI research studios, frontier model labs, and computational intelligence environments. Stripping away legacy teal/cyan scientific defaults, it channels the sleek, high-conviction minimalism of frontier developer ecosystems (Linear, Vercel, OpenAI) blended with cinematic deep-space instrumentation.

The interface evokes uncompromising computational rigor, speed, and luminous clarity. Clean obsidian surfaces recede into the background, allowing electric indigo and radiant violet accents to guide focus with laser accuracy. Translucent optical boundaries, ultra-fine 1px structural strokes, and high-frequency monospace readouts project institutional authority without bureaucratic weight.

## Colors
The color palette departs completely from legacy teals into an authoritative, futuristic tri-tier spectrum:

- **Primary (`#6366f1` / `#818cf8`)**: Electric Indigo. Acts as the primary operational catalyst, driving primary call-to-actions, active cluster nodes, highlighted code syntax, and dominant model states.
- **Secondary (`#a855f7`)**: Neon Violet. Used for secondary computational vectors, generative diffusion passes, token attention highlights, and atmospheric edge glows.
- **Tertiary (`#10b981` / `#34d399`)**: Refined Emerald. Reserved strictly for deterministic operational health: high-availability node states, benchmark performance gains, low latency verification, and connected streaming webhooks.
- **Neutrals**: Anchored by deep obsidian `#090b10` as canvas baseline, elevating to `#0f141f` for modular cards and `#161e2e` for elevated floating docks. Foreground typography relies on pure zinc `#f8fafc` with secondary slate `#94a3b8` to maintain pristine optical hierarchy without blinding contrast.

## Typography
Typography is strictly calibrated for rapid scanning and dense informational clarity:
- **Geist** provides an ultra-modern geometric foundation for interface copy, section titles, contextual menus, and narrative inspection reports. Tight negative letter-spacing on headline scales reflects contemporary developer tool standards.
- **JetBrains Mono** anchors telemetry figures, matrix representations, hyperparameter tokens, cluster IP addresses, and console logs. Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be active globally across monospace elements to prevent jitter during live inference telemetry.
